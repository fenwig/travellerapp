CREATION CODEX

Overview: Building Your Character

The Character Creation tool walks you through the process of building a Traveller character step by step. You'll roll characteristics, choose careers, face mishaps and events, gain skills and benefits, and ultimately produce a fully realized adventurer ready for play.

This guide explains what to expect at each stage and highlights important things to watch out for.

The Creation Journey

Step 1: Basic Information

Enter your character's name, species, and homeworld. These are personal details that set the stage for your character's story.

What happens: You'll also roll your initial characteristics (STR, DEX, END, INT, EDU, SOC) and optionally PSI if your species has it. These six (or seven) numbers are the foundation of your character's abilities.

Step 2: Pre-Career Events (Upbringing)

Before you enlist in a career, you'll experience life events that shape you. You might gain allies, rivals, contacts, or skills. You may also face injuries or other setbacks.

What happens: These events might modify your characteristics, add skills, or add allies and enemies to your life story.

Step 3: Career Terms (4+ Years Each)

The bulk of character creation involves serving career terms. You can serve multiple terms in the same career, switch careers, or leave to pursue other paths. Each term includes several phases:

Qualification: Roll to qualify for the career. Success lets you join; failure means you either wait a year or try a different career.

Assignment: Choose which role you fill (Army officer vs. enlisted soldier, for example).

Survival: Roll to survive the hazards of your career. You might face injury, ejection, or worse.

Events: Roll on your career's event table. You might gain skills, allies, equipment, or face new challenges.

Advancement: Roll to advance in rank within your career.

Skill Training: Gain new skills based on your rank and specialty.

Term End: Gain benefit rolls and mustering-out cash based on your rank.

Step 4: Post-Career Aging and Life Events

After all your career terms, you'll age and experience final life events that round out your character's story.

What to Watch For

Tracking Characteristics and Skills

⚠ IMPORTANT: Carefully monitor your characteristics and skills throughout creation!

Your characteristics (STR, DEX, END, INT, EDU, SOC, and PSI) will increase or decrease based on:

  • Career assignments (some roles provide stat increases at higher ranks)
  • Career events (you might gain or lose stats)
  • Injuries (you'll lose points if you're hurt — medical care can restore them)
  • Aging (after your final term, you age and lose points naturally)

Similarly, skills are added from:

  • Your career's training tables
  • Career events and upbringing
  • Mishaps and other special circumstances

The creation tool displays your current stats and skills in the panel at the top of each step. Check them frequently to ensure they're changing as expected. If something looks wrong, keep a note of it — you can fix it on the character sheet after creation is complete.

Discrepancies? No Problem

If you notice that a characteristic or skill didn't change when you expected it to — or changed by the wrong amount — don't panic. You can manually adjust any stat or skill on the Character Record page after creation is complete.

Simply note what should be corrected, finish creation, then edit the character sheet to fix it. It takes just a moment.

Benefit Rolls and Mustering Out

At the end of each career term, you receive benefit rolls — a chance to gain cash, equipment, allies, ranks, or other rewards. The number of rolls depends on your rank and rank achievements.

These rolls are important: they build your wealth and contacts. The creation tool will prompt you to spend each roll as you reach the end of a term. Pay attention to what you're gaining — you can always review your final character on the Character Record.

Tips for Success
  • Know your career. Each career has unique events, mishaps, and skill tables. Read the text carefully — it tells you what each roll means.
  • Injuries are serious. If you fail a survival roll and get hurt, you'll need medical care. The creation tool will offer it, but it costs money or adds debt.
  • Terms can end early. Mishaps or failed survival rolls can force you to leave a career. That's okay — you gain a benefit roll and can enlist elsewhere.
  • You can switch careers. If you're not happy with your first career, you can leave and join another. Each career gives you new opportunities and skills.
  • Watch the log. The creation log at the bottom-right tracks everything that happens — rolls, events, stat changes, skills gained. If something looks wrong, review the log to see exactly what occurred.
