NPC FLEET (GM)

The GM's ships. They have no Spacecraft Record and stay off the Roster and the players' pages. Each ship is fully staffed with NPC crew at skill level 1 (average characteristics, DM+0); use the + and − in a card's NPC setup to change their level. A ship added more than once is numbered: Yacht 1, Yacht 2. Turrets arrive empty, so pick their weapons in the card's NPC setup.
