EQUIPMENT

Augmentation Overview

Augments are cybernetic implants, biological modifications, or surgical alterations that permanently enhance a Traveller beyond their natural limits. While technology permits significant alteration, unnecessary augmentation is frowned upon in many cultures — and outright banned on others. Some worlds make augmentation mandatory for specific occupations.

All augments require surgery, typically 1D weeks of recovery. Augmentations can raise characteristics above normal maximums for a species. Core p.106 · CSC p.48

Medical Treatment Interference

Augments complicate medical care. Any long-term care or surgery Medic check treating an augmented Traveller suffers a negative DM equal to the difference in Tech Level between the medical facility and the highest relevant implant. A TL15 augment treated at a TL10 hospital imposes DM-5 on all surgery checks. Core p.107

Cognitive Augments

Replacing slow nerve cells with faster synthetic substrates and implanting optoelectronic boosters increases the speed at which a Traveller thinks — effectively boosting Intelligence. Core p.106 · CSC p.55

Sensory & Neural Augments

Enhanced Vision (TL13)

Cybernetic eye replacements providing binocular-equivalent magnification combined with IR and light-intensification capability — all hands-free and always active. Cost: Cr25,000.

Neural Comm

An implanted comm unit operated entirely by thought. Has identical capabilities to a standard comm but requires no hands and is undetectable. TL10 (audio only) Cr1,000 · TL12 (audio/visual, Computer/0) Cr5,000 · TL14 (full data, Computer/1) Cr20,000.

Wafer Jack (TL12)

A skull-mounted computer implant (Computer/2) for Expert software only, always running Intelligent Interface. The user accesses Expert programs by thought alone — no external device needed. Cannot run software beyond Expert packages. Cost: Cr10,000 (jack); Expert software purchased separately.

Skill Augmentation (TL12)

The Traveller's nervous system is rewired for one specific skill, granting DM+1 when using it. Requires the Traveller to already possess the skill at level 0+. A Traveller may only have one skill augmentation. Cost: Cr50,000.

Subdermal Armour

Adds a mesh of ballistic fibres to the skin and reinforces the bones. Subdermal armour stacks with all other protection — it is always active and cannot be removed by an attacker. Core p.107 · CSC p.56

Communications

Above TL8, being out of touch except in the most remote areas is essentially unthinkable. Communications technology merges steadily with computers — by TL10 a mobile comm is also a capable hand computer. Core p.108 · CSC p.62

Transceivers

Stand-alone radio or laser comms that work without a planetary network — direct point-to-point. Essential in wilderness, space, or worlds without infrastructure. 500km range is sufficient for reliable orbital contact.

Radio (TL5): Cr225–Cr15,000 · Laser (TL9): Cr500–Cr2,500. Higher TL reduces mass and integrates computer capability.

Commdot (TL10)

A tiny microphone/speaker/transmitter a few millimetres across. Interfaces with any other comm device within a few metres. Used as hands-free earpieces, throat mics, or improvised bugs. Cost: Cr10.

Bugs (TL5–15)

Hidden surveillance devices. Can be passive (record until collected) or active (transmit continuously). Range grows shorter the smaller the bug. TL14 bugs can be the size of a dust mote. Cost: Cr50–Cr500. Capabilities expand from audio-only up to bioscan and onboard Computer/1 at high TL.

Computers & Software

How Computers Work

A computer's rating (e.g. Computer/3) is its Processing score — its ability to run software. Storage capacity isn't tracked; beyond TL8 it's effectively unlimited.

Every piece of software has a Bandwidth cost. At any one time, a computer can run any combination of software whose combined Bandwidth doesn't exceed its Processing score. A Computer/3 could run Translator/1 and Security/2 together, but swapping in something else first requires freeing up Bandwidth. A Processing 0 computer can only run one Bandwidth 0 package at a time.

Software can run at a lower effective Bandwidth than its rating, down to the lowest Bandwidth version available — e.g. Intrusion/3 can run on a Computer/1, but only functions as Intrusion/1.

Tech Level is a hard gate, separate from Bandwidth: a computer must be of a TL equal to or greater than the software's TL to run it at all, regardless of spare Bandwidth. Software above Bandwidth 1 also can't be copied easily — it takes meaningful Bandwidth just to transfer it. Core p.110

Space is actively hostile to human life. Survival gear covers the spectrum from a simple filter mask to pressurised habitat modules capable of sustaining a six-person crew for a week. See also Vacc Suits and HEV Suits in Weapons & Armor. Core p.118 · CSC p.97

Field Equipment

Grav Belt (TL12)

A harness fitted with artificial gravity modules — allows free flight at Medium speed. Internal battery lasts 4 hours before recharging (12 hours at TL15). Cost: Cr100,000.

Rescue Bubble (TL9)

A 2-metre emergency life raft inflated by a small oxygen tank, providing 2 person/hours of life support. Piezoelectric walls convert movement to power for a distress beacon. Found on spacecraft and sea vessels. Cost: Cr600.

Habitat Module (TL8–10)

Modular quarters for 6 people. TL8 is unpressurised — withstands near-hurricane winds, includes survival rations and a week of battery power, requires 12 man-hours to assemble. TL10 version is pressurised with life support for one week. Modules link together. Cost: Cr10,000–Cr20,000.

Portable Fusion Generator (TL10)

Light-duty fusion generator capable of recharging weapons, suits, and other equipment for up to one month of use. Essential for extended wilderness operations with energy-hungry gear. Mass: 20kg. Cost: Cr500,000.

Tent (TL3–7)

Basic shelter for two against weather. TL7 version can be pressurised (no airlock — depressurises when opened). Cost: Cr200–Cr2,000.

Tool Laser

Tool Laser (TL12)

A handheld laser welder/cutting torch powered by an internal pack (up to 20 minutes continuous use). Variable output from fine electronic welding to cutting through several centimetres of steel — heavier work requires a dedicated welding kit. At TL12 it's flashlight-sized; TL13+ it fits in a pen. Can be used as a weapon (DM-2 to attacks, 5m range, 1D damage, burns 1 minute per shot). Cost: Cr500–Cr750. CSC p.130
