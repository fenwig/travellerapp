SKILLS & TASKS

Core Mechanic

Making a Check
2D + Skill Level + Characteristic DM + other DMs ≥ Target Number

Roll 2D and add your skill level and the relevant characteristic DM. If the total meets or exceeds the target number, you succeed. The referee should only call for checks when the Travellers are in danger, the task is especially difficult or hazardous, they are under time pressure, or success and failure are both interesting outcomes.

If no difficulty is specified, assume the task is Average (8+).

Boon, Bane & Effect

Boon

Roll an extra die and discard the lowest. Granted when a Traveller has helpful circumstances — good tools, assistance, favourable conditions.

Bane

Roll an extra die and discard the highest. Applied when hindered — poor tools, darkness, distractions, adverse conditions.

A Boon and a Bane on the same check cancel out. Multiple Boons or Banes still only count as one.

Effect

Effect = Total rolled − Target Number. Measures how well (or badly) a Traveller performed. Used to calculate combat damage and task chain bonuses.

EffectResultMeaning
−6 or lessExceptional FailureEverything that can go wrong does. Devices damaged, situations made worse.
−2 to −5Average FailureThe task simply fails.
−1Marginal FailureAlmost succeeded. Referee may allow scraping a success with a significant cost.
0Marginal SuccessBarely succeeds — may need to accept a condition or complication.
1 to 5Average SuccessThe task succeeds normally.
6+Exceptional SuccessSucceeds in an impressive and elegant fashion.

Special Check Types

Opposed Checks

When two characters directly oppose each other, both make their checks normally. The winner is the one with the highest Effect. On a draw, neither gains an advantage — they may try again or events overtake them.

Multiple Tasks

Doing two or more things at once — firing ship weapons while also flying, or disarming a bomb while hiding — raises the difficulty of each task by one level for every extra task being performed simultaneously. An Average task becomes Difficult; a Difficult task becomes Very Difficult, and so on.

Timeframes

Each task has a random timeframe of 1–6 time units. The referee sets the increment based on the nature of the task.

Going faster: DM−2  |  Going slower: DM+2  |  One step only

Before rolling you may shift one step up (faster, DM-2) or one step down (slower, DM+2). You cannot shift more than one step.

Task Chains

When tasks are linked — the result of one check influences the next — the Effect of the previous check applies as a DM to the next check in the chain. Chains can involve multiple Travellers working together or one Traveller performing a series of related actions.

Previous CheckDM to Next Check
Failed — Effect −6 or lessDM−3
Failed — Effect −2 to −5DM−2
Failed — Effect −1DM−1
Succeeded — Effect 0DM+1
Succeeded — Effect 1 to 5DM+2
Succeeded — Effect 6+DM+3

Working Together: When multiple Travellers work on the same task simultaneously, one makes the final check while others make supporting checks first. Each supporter's Effect is consulted on the Task Chain table and that DM is applied to the lead Traveller's check.

Combat Sequence

Round Structure

Combat is divided into rounds of approximately 6 seconds. At the start of combat, each side makes an Initiative check — Dexterity + Tactics (military) if trained, or straight DEX DM if not. The Effect of the winning side's check is added to every combatant on that side as a bonus to their Initiative score. Combatants then act in order from highest Initiative to lowest. Every combatant retains their Initiative score for the entire combat.

Initiative = 2D + Tactics (military) + INT or DEX DM

Attack Rolls

Melee Attack
2D + Melee (speciality) + STR DM

Target number is Average (8+). On a hit, add STR DM and Effect to the weapon's damage roll.

Ranged Attack
2D + Gun Combat (speciality) + DEX DM

Target number is Average (8+). Some weapons use Heavy Weapons or Athletics (dexterity) in place of Gun Combat.

Reactions

Reactions are performed outside your normal turn when you are about to be attacked. You may make unlimited Reactions each round, but each one applies DM−1 to your next set of actions.

Dodge

Inflicts a penalty to the attacker's roll equal to your DEX DM or Athletics (dexterity), whichever is higher. Works against both ranged and melee attacks. Each attack must be dodged separately.

Parry

In close combat only. Inflicts your Melee skill level as a negative DM to the attacker's attack roll. Pistols can also be parried in melee, representing the weapon being knocked aside.

Dive for Cover

Ranged attacks only. You throw yourself to the ground, inflicting DM−2 on every attacker targeting you this round. You may dive behind any object within 1.5 metres and gain its cover Protection bonus. If no cover is nearby, you only gain DM−1 (prone target). Cost: you forfeit your next actions entirely.

Damage & Armour

Damage
Damage = Weapon dice + Effect of attack roll  (+ STR DM for melee)

Damage is applied to END first. If END reaches 0, excess damage goes to either STR or DEX (target's choice). If either STR or DEX reaches 0, the Traveller falls unconscious and further damage drains the remaining characteristic. If all three physical characteristics reach 0, the Traveller is killed.

Recalculate characteristic DMs whenever a characteristic changes due to damage.

Armour

Armour's Protection score is subtracted from damage before it is applied. An attack with Effect 6+ always inflicts at least 1 point of damage regardless of Protection. Weapons with the AP trait ignore a portion of armour equal to their AP score.

Special Situations
Melee & Close Combat Rules ▼

When a Traveller is within 2 metres of an enemy they are in close combat. The following rules apply:

Melee attacksFreely available to both combatants.
Target restrictionNeither combatant may attack anyone other than those they are locked with.
Single-handed ranged weaponsPistols may be fired in close combat, but can be parried.
Large weaponsRifles and two-handed weapons may only be used as clubs.
Moving out of close combatGrants the enemy an immediate free attack at DM+2.
Grappling ▼

To grapple, make an opposed Melee (unarmed) check using either STR or DEX DM. The winner may choose one of the following:

Force opponent prone
Disarm opponentEffect 6+: you may take the weapon.
Throw opponent1D metres, 1D damage. Ends the grapple.
Inflict damage2 + Effect of the check, ignores armour.
Attack with pistol or small bladeNormal weapon damage.
EscapeMove away as a normal movement action.
Drag opponentUp to 3 metres.
Continue grappleNo other effect.

While grappling, neither combatant may perform any Significant or Minor Action except further opposed Melee (unarmed) checks.

Dual Weapons ▼

A Traveller may wield two single-handed weapons simultaneously (two blades, two pistols, or a blade and pistol). They may attack with both in the same round, but:

  • They may not aim with either weapon.
  • Both attack rolls suffer DM−2.
  • The penalty does not apply if only one weapon is actually used to attack that round.
Extended Actions in Combat ▼

Some skill checks take longer than one combat round — hacking a terminal, repairing an engine, downloading data under fire. These use the normal Timeframe rules. If the Traveller takes damage while performing an Extended Action:

  • They must immediately make a check using the same skill they are performing.
  • The damage taken is applied as a negative DM to that check.
  • Failure: this round's progress does not count.
  • Failure with Effect −6 or worse: the task is ruined and must be restarted from scratch.
