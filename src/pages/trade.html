TRADE

Passage Types

High Passage

Luxury cruise equivalent. Comfortable stateroom, good food, attentive service. Requires one stateroom, one ton of cargo space, and Steward skill 1 per 10 passengers.

Middle Passage

Standard class — functional but not enjoyable. One stateroom per passenger, occasional steward service. Requires one stateroom, 100 kg cargo space, Steward 1 per 100 passengers.

Basic Passage

Steerage. Staterooms shared (two per room) or basic cargo-bay accommodation with minimal facilities. Requires two tons of spare room. 10 kg baggage allowance. No steward required.

Low Passage

Cryogenic freezing for the journey. Risk of death from system shock, improper revival, or berth malfunction. Requires one low berth. 10 kg baggage allowance.

Operating with Passengers
Working Passage

Identical to middle passage but the passenger pays their way by serving in a crew role for the duration of the trip. Baggage allowance 100 kg. Core p.158

Middle Passage — Stand-by Rules

Middle passage is sold on a stand-by basis — middle passengers can be bumped by high passengers who book later. Many captains only take middle passengers if they cannot fill staterooms with high passage clients or lack enough stewards.

A Traveller with Steward 0 (not Steward 1) can provide suitable service for up to 10 middle passengers. Core p.158

Low Berth Revival

When a low berth is opened, the medic must make a revival check. On a failure, the passenger does not survive.

Low Passage Lottery

It is customary for the captain to contribute Cr10 from every low passage ticket into a lottery pool. Each low passenger guesses how many will survive the trip. The winner collects the pot — but if the winner is among the dead, the captain keeps the prize. Administered by the ship's steward. Core p.158

Freight Overview

Shipping freight pays a fixed rate per ton (see Passengers tab for the rate table). Freight lots must be transported in their entirety — they cannot be broken up. Payment is made on delivery.

Late delivery: reduces payment by 1D + 4 × 10%

Major Cargo Lot

1D × 10 tons of freight.

Minor Cargo Lot

1D × 5 tons of freight.

Incidental Cargo Lot

1D tons of freight.

Trade Checklist

  1. Find a supplier or local broker
  2. Determine goods available
  3. Determine purchase price
  4. Purchase goods
  5. Travel to another market
  6. Find a buyer or local broker
  7. Determine sale price

Speculative trade is the path to great fortunes — and bankruptcy. The greatest profits and losses are here. Big shipping corporations don't follow these rules — they have government contracts, bulk ships, and bribes. Independent traders live and die by the dice.

On worlds regularly visited by trade vessels, suppliers are easy to find. Travellers can search for multiple suppliers but suffer DM−1 per previous attempt on the same planet in the same month.

Local Brokers

Legal Broker

Broker skill = 2D÷3. Gains DM+2 on negotiations (local knowledge). Charges 10% of gross proceeds.

Black Market Broker / Fixer

Streetwise skill = 2D÷3. Gains DM+2 on negotiations. Charges 20% of gross proceeds (higher for very dangerous cargo). If skill roll is a natural 2, the fixer is an informer or government agent.

Determining Goods Available

Common Goods can be purchased on any world. Trade Goods are available only on worlds with the matching Trade code. A supplier will have:

  • All Common Goods
  • All Trade Goods matching the world's Trade codes
  • A number of randomly determined goods equal to the world's Population code

Roll on the Trade Goods table to determine random goods; reroll results 61–65 unless dealing with a black market supplier.

Population affects quantity: Population 3 or less applies DM−3 to quantity rolls (can result in zero). Population 9+ applies DM+3.

Core p.242

Purchase Price

Roll 3D and apply the following modifiers. Consult the Modified Price table for the purchase percentage of base price.

3D + Broker skill + Purchase DM − Sale DM − Supplier's Broker skill (default 2)

If multiple Purchase or Sale DMs apply, use only the largest from each column. The trader may reject the price but cannot deal with that supplier again for at least a month.

Sale Price

Find a buyer using the same rules as finding a supplier. Roll 3D with this formula:

3D + Broker skill + Sale DM − Purchase DM − Purchaser's Broker skill (default 2)

If the trader rejects the offered price, they must find another buyer or wait a month. Core p.243

Illegal Goods & Smuggling

Some goods are universally illegal (marked on the Trade Goods table) and carry high Sale DMs. Other goods are illegal only on specific worlds based on their Law Level.

Law Level Sale DM for locally banned goods: Law Level of world − Law Level the goods are banned at. For example, Military Weapons are banned at Law Level 3. On a Law Level 9 world, Sale DM = +6.

If goods are both universally and locally illegal, use whichever Sale DM is higher. Black market suppliers hold whatever illegal goods match the world's Trade codes plus randomly rolled illegal goods.

Caught smuggling? See Law & Sentencing rules in General Rules. Fines are per ton of contraband. Core p.242, 255–256
