VEHICLES

Speed Bands

Vehicle speed is measured in bands. The Speed Band Number is used in collision calculations. A vehicle may change speed by one band per combat round at Fast or below, or by one band per minute if travelling faster. Travellers on foot use Idle. Core Rulebook p.136

Cruising Speed

A vehicle's listed Speed is its maximum. Most vehicles cruise at one Speed Band lower to balance speed and endurance. Cruising speed = maximum Speed − 1 band. At cruising speed, the vehicle's Range increases by 50%. Vehicle stat blocks show both values as Speed (Cruise). Core Rulebook p.136

Rough Terrain

Listed speeds assume relatively flat or even surfaces — roads, tracks, short grass. Core Rulebook p.136

Airborne Movement

Aircraft (Flyer skill)

Designed for a specific atmosphere and planetary gravity. An aircraft can only function on worlds with Size and Atmosphere codes within ±2 of its world of origin.

Outside that range: DM−1 to all control checks. If Size or Atmosphere is beyond range: cannot fly at all. Minimum Atmosphere 1 required.

Grav Vehicles (Flyer grav)

Anti-gravity technology — unaffected by the Atmosphere and Size of any world. Grav vehicles operate on any world regardless of atmosphere or gravity.

Core Rulebook p.136

Equipment Descriptions

Autopilot

At TL5, autopilots function only on aircraft and sea vessels; available to all vehicles at TL9. Allows fully autonomous operation. Treated as Drive, Flyer or Seafarer skill level 0, increasing by +1 every two Tech Levels to a maximum of 3. Cost is +Cr5,000 per skill level above 0.

Communications

Standard range is 5km. Range can be multiplied by itself by doubling the cost. TL8+ systems can communicate with overhead satellites and use lasers or masers for secure, un-interceptable signals.

Navigation

Basic: DM+1 to all Navigation checks. Improved: DM+2. Advanced: DM+3.

Off-Road Modification

Any Drive skill vehicle may be modified to go off-road. Lowers maximum Speed by one Speed Band. Costs 50% of the vehicle's original purchase price. Vehicles must be designed with aquatic drives in mind — cannot be retrofitted.

Sensors

Basic sensors have a range of 1km. For every 3 Tech Levels above TL5, increase sensor cost by +50%. Improved: DM+1 to Electronics (sensors), range 5km. Advanced: DM+2 to Electronics (sensors), range 25km.

Short Term Life Support

Provides a breathable atmosphere inside a closed vehicle for one day regardless of exterior conditions. Cost is per person supported.

Turret

Allows a vehicle-mounted weapon to track any target within line of sight, removing fire arc restrictions. Turret mass equals the weapons it holds. Cost is Cr10,000 per ton. A vehicle may mount one weapon per 10 points of Hull (or part thereof).

Damaging Vehicles

Subtract the vehicle's Armour from the attack's total damage. The remainder is removed from the vehicle's Hull. Hull 0 = vehicle is wrecked — totally inoperable, normally beyond repair, though parts may be salvaged. Core Rulebook p.139

Hull damage = Weapon damage + Effect − Vehicle Armour

Vehicle Armour

Vehicles have armour on different facings. The armour used is always from the facing the attack came from.

FacingArmour Value
Front / Side / RearAs listed in vehicle stat block
RoofHalf of the side armour value
FloorHalf of the rear armour value

Light Weapons

Against any weapon with less than Damage 4D or the Stun trait, a vehicle gains extra Armour equal to its Tech Level on every facing.

Example: TL8 groundcar (Armour 2) vs. a rifle (3D) → effective Armour 10. Against a PGMP (12D) → normal Armour 2.

Critical Hits

A critical hit occurs when an attack has Effect 6 or higher and causes damage. Roll 2D on the Critical Hits Location table, then consult the Critical Hit Effects table. Core Rulebook p.140

Critical hit when: Effect ≥ 6 and damage is caused
Severity = Effect of attack − 5
Sustained Damage

Every time a vehicle loses Hull equal to 10% of its starting Hull score, roll 2D on the Critical Hits Location table and apply a Severity 1 critical hit.

Repeat Hits to Same Location

Use whichever is higher: the new Severity or the existing Severity +1. Once a location reaches Severity 6, it takes no more critical hits — instead the vehicle takes 6D extra damage each time that location would be hit again.

Repairs

Repairing a vehicle requires a facility with suitable maintenance capabilities. Core Rulebook p.141

Hull Damage
Average (8+) Mechanic check (1D hours + 1 per point of damage, INT or EDU)

Spare parts are consumed at Cr500 per point of Hull restored.

Critical Hits
Average (8+) Mechanic check (1D hours, INT or EDU)

The Severity of the critical hit is applied as a negative DM to the repair check. Each critical hit is repaired separately.

Destroyed Equipment

Any weapons or equipment destroyed by critical hits cannot be repaired — they must be completely replaced at full purchase cost.
