TRAVELLER

PIRACY ENCOUNTER

Pirates of Drinax, Book 1, Chapter 3 (pp.13–23). Step 1: describe the system and find the prey. Step 2: Find Prey picks a ship of the right kind from the ship list (docs/Traveller Ships.xlsx) and fills in its details; change anything you like, then build the encounter: morale, distance, travel times, quirks, complications, the first pirate hunter response, cargo and loot. Results are shown only on this screen and are kept in this browser, not shared with other players. Cargo uses the house rule: 40% common goods, 40% trade goods, and 20% goods for the trade route you choose (or more trade goods if there is no route).
