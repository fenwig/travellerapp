GENERAL RULES

Core Mechanic

Making a Check
2D + Skill Level + Characteristic DM + other DMs ≥ Target Number

Roll 2D and add your skill level and the relevant characteristic DM. If the total meets or exceeds the target number, you succeed. The referee should only call for checks when the Travellers are in danger, the task is especially difficult or hazardous, they are under time pressure, or success and failure are both interesting outcomes.

If no difficulty is specified, assume the task is Average (8+).

Boon, Bane & Effect

Boon

Roll an extra die and discard the lowest. Granted when a Traveller has helpful circumstances — good tools, assistance, favourable conditions.

Bane

Roll an extra die and discard the highest. Applied when hindered — poor tools, darkness, distractions, adverse conditions.

A Boon and a Bane on the same check cancel out. Multiple Boons or Banes still only count as one.

Effect

Effect = Total rolled − Target Number. Measures how well (or badly) a Traveller performed. Used to calculate combat damage and task chain bonuses.

EffectResultMeaning
−6 or lessExceptional FailureEverything that can go wrong does. Devices damaged, situations made worse.
−2 to −5Average FailureThe task simply fails.
−1Marginal FailureAlmost succeeded. Referee may allow scraping a success with a significant cost.
0Marginal SuccessBarely succeeds — may need to accept a condition or complication.
1 to 5Average SuccessThe task succeeds normally.
6+Exceptional SuccessSucceeds in an impressive and elegant fashion.

Special Check Types

Opposed Checks

When two characters directly oppose each other, both make their checks normally. The winner is the one with the highest Effect. On a draw, neither gains an advantage — they may try again or events overtake them.

Multiple Tasks

Doing two or more things at once raises the difficulty of each task by one level for every extra task being performed simultaneously.

When tasks are linked, the Effect of the previous check applies as a DM to the next check in the chain.

Working Together: Each supporter makes a check first; their Effect is looked up on the Task Chain table and that DM is applied to the lead Traveller's check.

Environmental Dangers

Full tables: Core Rulebook p.80–82.

Disease & Poison

Make an END check at the listed difficulty. On failure, take the listed damage and repeat after the interval. p.80

Falling

1D damage per 2 metres fallen on a 1G world. A successful Athletics check reduces the effective distance by the Effect. Armour does not protect against falling damage. p.80

Fatigue

Fatigued Travellers suffer DM−2 to all checks until they rest. p.80

Radiation

Measured in rads — cumulative and permanent until treated. Effects range from nausea (51–150 rads) to death (800+ rads). p.81

Suffocation

Without life support: 1D damage per minute. Without any air: 1D damage per round. p.81

Vacuum

Cumulative damage each round — 1D in round 1, 2D in round 2, 3D in round 3, and so on. p.81

Temperature

Extreme heat and cold deal damage to END — from 1D/hour in arctic to 3D/round in an inferno. p.82

Gravity

High gravity (1.4G+): DM−1 to all checks until acclimatised. Low/Zero-G: DM−1 to all physical checks; firing recoil weapons requires Athletics (dexterity) Average (8+). p.80

Medical Treatment

First Aid
Medic (EDU) check — restores characteristic points equal to Effect (minimum 1)

Must be initiated within one minute of the injury. Requires at least a basic medikit. Can only be successfully applied once per injury.

Medical Care
3 + END DM + Medic skill = characteristic points restored per day

Available when only one or two physical characteristics are damaged. Requires a hospital or sickbay and full bed rest.

Surgery

Required when all three physical characteristics are damaged. Restores points equal to the Effect of a Medic (EDU) check.

Warning: a failed surgery check causes the patient to lose an additional 3 + Effect characteristic points.

Augmentation & Medical Care

All Medic checks suffer a negative DM equal to the difference in Tech Level between the medical facility and the Traveller's highest relevant implant.

Natural Healing

Normal Rest
1D + END DM per day of full rest
After Surgery
END DM only per day of rest

If END DM is negative, the Traveller continues to deteriorate without medical care.

Unconsciousness

Make an END check after every minute. Success means the Traveller regains consciousness. Each failed check grants a cumulative DM+1 to the next attempt.

Mental Characteristics

INT and EDU damage heals at 1 point per characteristic per day unless otherwise specified.
