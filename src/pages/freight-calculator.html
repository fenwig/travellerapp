TRAVELLER

FREIGHT & MAIL CALCULATOR

Freight (Core Rulebook pp.239–241): freight lots cannot be broken up, so tick the whole lots you want to carry. Cargo is paid on delivery; late delivery cuts the payment by (1D+4) × 10%. Mail (p.241): roll 2D plus the freight modifiers; on 12 or more you are offered 1D containers (5 tons and Cr25000 each, flat rate). Take all of them or none.
