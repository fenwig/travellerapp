COMBAT

Combat Sequence

Round Structure

Combat is divided into rounds of approximately 6 seconds. At the start of combat, every Traveller individually makes a DEX or INT check (whichever they prefer). House rule: rather than using the Effect, the number rolled is their place in the initiative order, with higher numbers acting first. Combatants act in order from highest to lowest.

Initiative = 2D + DEX or INT DM
Tactics (Optional)

Separately, one Traveller per side may make a Tactics check. The result compared to 8 (positive or negative) becomes a DM applied to every combatant on that side's initiative roll — a good Tactics check speeds up the whole side, a bad one slows it down.

Ambush

If one side is unaware of the other's presence when combat begins, the aware side gets DM+6 and the unaware side gets DM−6 to their initiative checks. This only applies for the first round of combat.

Attack Rolls

Melee Attack
2D + Melee (speciality) + STR DM

Target number is Average (8+). On a hit, add STR DM and Effect to the weapon's damage roll.

Ranged Attack
2D + Gun Combat (speciality) + DEX DM

Target number is Average (8+). Some weapons use Heavy Weapons or Athletics (dexterity).

Reactions

Reactions are performed outside your normal turn. Each one applies DM−1 to your next set of actions.

Dodge

Inflicts a penalty to the attacker's roll equal to your DEX DM or Athletics (dexterity), whichever is higher.

Parry

In close combat only. Inflicts your Melee skill level as a negative DM to the attacker's roll.

Dive for Cover

Ranged attacks only. Inflicts DM−2 on every attacker targeting you this round. You may dive behind any object within 1.5 metres. Cost: you forfeit your next actions entirely.

Damage & Armour

Damage
Damage = Weapon dice + Effect of attack roll  (+ STR DM for melee)

Damage is applied to END first. If END reaches 0, excess damage goes to either STR or DEX (target's choice). If either reaches 0, the Traveller falls unconscious. If all three reach 0, the Traveller is killed.

Armour

Armour's Protection score is subtracted from damage before it is applied. An attack with Effect 6+ always inflicts at least 1 point of damage. Weapons with the AP trait ignore a portion of armour equal to their AP score.

When a Traveller is within 2 metres of an enemy they are in close combat.

Dual Weapons ▼

A Traveller may wield two single-handed weapons simultaneously. Both attack rolls suffer DM−2. They may not aim with either weapon.

Extended Actions in Combat ▼

If a Traveller takes damage while performing an Extended Action, they must immediately make a check using the same skill. The damage taken is applied as a negative DM. Failure with Effect −6 or worse: the task is ruined and must be restarted.

Vehicles in Combat

Vehicles move on the driver's Initiative. The driver must spend a Minor Action each round for normal control (straight road, simple manoeuvres). Navigating obstacles, conducting evasion or pursuing a target requires a Significant Action. Core Rulebook p.138

Closed Vehicle

Occupants inside a closed vehicle cannot be directly attacked — they are not visible to attackers outside.

Passengers may fire out, but only through available windows or firing ports. Up to 2 people per fire arc in a civilian vehicle; 1 per arc in a military vehicle.

Mounted weapons cannot attack targets within 5% of their range.

Open Vehicle

Passengers in open vehicles can be directly attacked and have no cover from the vehicle itself.

Passengers may fire in any direction without restriction.

Vehicular Actions

All of the following are Significant Actions for the driver. All skill checks use the vehicle's Agility as a DM.

Dogfight

Requires both vehicles to be within one kilometre and one Speed Band of each other. Both drivers make opposed skill checks. The winner chooses which fire arc their vehicle sits in on the enemy, and which fire arc the enemy is in on their own vehicle. Winner gains DM+2 to all attack rolls that round; loser suffers DM−2. A draw means neither may attack unless they have a turret.

If the dogfight continues, the previous winner applies the difference from last round's check as a positive DM to this round.

Evasive Action

The driver makes a skill check. The Effect acts as a negative DM to all attacks against the vehicle and its passengers that round. The same Effect also penalises any attacks made from the vehicle — the sudden movement disrupts outgoing fire too.

Manoeuvre

Basic vehicle control — no skill check required. Avoid large or obvious obstacles, move to destination, shift out of one enemy fire arc into another, or change which fire arc a target is in. More extreme manoeuvres require Stunt.

Ram

Deliberately driving into another vehicle or obstacle. Requires a successful skill check. Affected by Dodge and Evasive Action as normal. The referee may grant bonuses or declare the attempt automatic if the target is very large or stationary. On success, apply Collision damage.

Stunt

A successful skill check lets the driver perform dramatic manoeuvres — two wheels, stunning aerobatics, skimming over obstacles. Can place a target in an additional fire arc, set up a task chain, or achieve up to three Manoeuvre actions at once. Referee sets difficulty based on the action attempted.

Weave

In obstacle-heavy environments (city streets, tunnels), the driver chooses a weaving score from 1 to their current Speed Band Number, applied as a negative DM to their own skill check. Failure = crashed into an obstacle. Success forces any pursuers to match the same check at the same penalty, or break off pursuit.

Vehicle Weapons

Attacking with ranged weapons mounted on a vehicle uses the same rules as normal combat with the following exceptions. Core Rulebook p.139

Space combat between spacecraft is a distinct system from personal and vehicle combat — using six-minute rounds, Range Bands measured in thousands of kilometres, crew duty roles, and specialised weapons and damage rules.
