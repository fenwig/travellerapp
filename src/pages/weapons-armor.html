WEAPONS & ARMOR

Armor Rules

Armor reduces damage by its Protection value before it affects characteristics. Only one suit of armor can normally be worn at a time. Resolve damage from the outside in — outer armor takes the hit first, then inner.

Some armors require a skill. Missing a required skill level inflicts DM-1 per missing level on all checks. No skill at all imposes the usual DM-3 unskilled penalty.

Worn armor has its mass reduced by 75% for encumbrance — it is distributed around the body. Core p.99 · CSC p.12

Armor Notes

Reflec vs Ablat

Reflec (+10 vs lasers) is expensive, hard to obtain, and imposes DM-2 on Stealth. Ablat is a cheap alternative — each laser hit reduces its laser protection by 1 as the material vaporises. Ablat can be worn over other armour.

Vacc Suit & Combat Armour

Both provide life support for 6 hours. Combat Armour functions as a full vacc suit. HEV Suits are for truly hostile environments — toxic atmospheres, deep underwater, extreme radiation. All weapons gain the Scope trait while wearing Combat Armour or Battle Dress.

Battle Dress

Powered full-body armour with servomotors granting STR +4, DEX +4. Includes Computer/2, 6-hour air supply, and full NBC protection. The TL14 version upgrades to Computer/3 and STR +6. Very bulky — requires Vacc Suit 2 (TL13) or Vacc Suit 1 (TL14).

Jack

Leather jacket or bodysuit. Useful against blades, near-useless against guns. The baseline for any Traveller who wants to be slightly less dead in a knife fight. Widely available and inconspicuous.

Melee Weapons Overview

Melee weapons use the Melee skill with three specialities: Blade for edged weapons, Bludgeon for blunt weapons, and Unarmed for fists and knuckles.

Melee weapons remain relevant at all tech levels. In boarding actions, a sword is far less likely to breach a hull or rupture a vacc suit than a gun — and a blade is very effective at opening an enemy's suit to vacuum. Nobility on many worlds carry blades as status symbols. Core p.124

Slug Throwers Overview

Despite advances in energy weapons, accelerating a small piece of metal to high velocity remains one of the most efficient ways to kill someone. Slug throwers use the Gun Combat (slug) skill.

Chemical propellants give way to gyrojet (rocket bullets, Zero-G trait) and gauss (electromagnetic rails, AP trait) at higher tech levels. Core p.126 · CSC p.142

Energy Weapons Overview

Energy weapons use the Gun Combat (energy) skill. All come with a power pack that can be recharged from a ship's power plant or other power source. Recharging takes 8 hours; switching power packs is a Minor Action.

All energy weapons have the Zero-G trait — no recoil. Laser weapons are countered by Reflec and Ablat armour. At high TL, power packs become integrated into the weapon itself. Core p.129 · CSC p.151

Heavy Weapons Overview

These are among the most powerful weapons to appear on the battlefield. All require the Heavy Weapons (portable) skill. Most are Bulky or Very Bulky — a gyrostabiliser (Cr500) removes Bulky but cannot be added to Destructive weapons.

PGHP and FGHP weapons are so heavy they can only be used comfortably by a Traveller in battle dress. The FGHP emits lethal radiation with each shot — anyone nearby without rad protection will suffer a potentially lethal dose. Core p.133 · CSC p.159

Artillery and vehicle-mounted weapons are out of scope for personal use — see Vehicles.

Explosives Rules

All explosives use the Explosives skill. The damage is multiplied by the Effect of the Explosives check used to set it. Effect 0 counts as Effect 1. Core p.134

Damage × Effect of Explosives check

Doubling the quantity of explosive increases damage by 1D and Blast by +1. This stacks — quadruple quantity adds 2D damage and Blast +2. Eight times adds 3D and Blast +3.

Destructive Weapons

Some weapons are so powerful they can blast apart small vehicles with a single shot. These are referred to as Destructive and are noted in their Damage score with two Ds — for example, 3DD. Core p.78

Destructive damage = dice rolled × 10

A Gyrostabiliser cannot be added to any Destructive weapon. PGHP and FGHP weapons are both Destructive.

Weapon Traits

Traits are special rules that significantly affect how a weapon is used. They appear in every weapon's stat line and are defined here. Core p.79

TraitRule
AP X Armour Piercing — ignores an amount of Protection equal to X. Has no effect against spacecraft-scale targets unless the weapon is also spacecraft-scale.
Auto X Fires multiple rounds per trigger pull. Three modes:
Single — normal attack rules.
Burst — add Auto score to damage; uses Auto score rounds.
Full Auto — make Auto score separate attacks against targets within 6m of each other; uses 3× Auto score rounds.
Cannot combine Auto with Scope or an aiming action.
Blast X Affects every target within X metres of the impact point. No Dodge Reactions allowed — targets may only Dive for Cover. Cover between target and blast centre applies.
Bulky Requires STR 9+ to use without penalty. Below STR 9, attack rolls suffer a negative DM equal to the difference between the Traveller's STR DM and +1. A Gyrostabiliser (Cr500, TL9) removes this trait.
Radiation Target receives 2D×20 rads on a hit (2D×60 for spacecraft-scale weapons). If the weapon is also Destructive, every target within 10 metres receives 2D×20 rads as well.
Scope Vision-enhancing sights fitted to the weapon. Normally all attacks beyond 100m are automatically Extreme Range — Scope removes this limit, provided the Traveller aims before shooting.
Smart Guided rounds that home in on a target. Gain a DM to attack equal to the difference between the weapon's TL and the target's TL (usually their armour or vehicle). Minimum DM+1, maximum DM+6.
Stun Non-lethal. Damage is only applied to END (after armour). If END reaches 0, the target is incapacitated for a number of rounds equal to the excess damage. Stun damage is fully healed after one hour of rest.
Very Bulky Requires STR 12+ to use without penalty. Below STR 12, attack rolls suffer a negative DM equal to the difference between the Traveller's STR DM and +2. A Gyrostabiliser cannot be added to Destructive weapons.
Zero-G Little or no recoil. Can be used in low or zero gravity without requiring an Athletics (dexterity) check.

Additional Traits CSC p.131

The Central Supply Catalog introduces additional traits used by weapons in its expanded listings.

TraitRule
Dangerous This weapon can be lethal to its user. If an attack roll results in Effect −5 or worse, the weapon explodes — damage is inflicted on the firer and the weapon is destroyed.
Fire Sets the target on fire, causing damage every round after the initial attack. Only one Fire weapon's damage applies at a time (use the highest). A fire extinguishes itself on 2D 8+ at the start of each round, or a Traveller can spend a Significant Action and pass an Average (8+) DEX check to put it out (DM+2 with firefighting equipment).
One Use The weapon is completely expended after a single use and rendered inoperable. Disposable launchers and single-shot devices use this trait.
Silent The weapon channels or removes the excess sound produced when firing. Any attempt to detect the source of the shot suffers DM−6.
Smasher Extremely heavy and carries great momentum when swung. A target attacked by a Smasher weapon may not attempt to parry it.
Very Dangerous Only a madman uses this weapon. If an attack roll results in Effect −3 or worse, the weapon explodes — damage is inflicted on the firer and the weapon is destroyed.
