SPACECRAFT COMBAT

Round Structure

Space combat uses a six-minute combat round (rather than six seconds used for personal and vehicle combat). Each Traveller on board takes up a duty position and acts once per round. When ships are at Close range (10km or less) or directly engaging vehicles or Travellers, switch to the Dogfighting rules using six-second rounds. Core Rulebook p.164

Combat Steps (each round, in order)
1. Manoeuvre StepIn Initiative order — each ship allocates Thrust to movement or combat manoeuvring.
2. Attack StepIn Initiative order — each ship fires weapons or conducts boarding actions.
3. Actions StepIn Initiative order — crew perform miscellaneous actions: repairs, jumping, reloading, electronic warfare.

Initiative

2D + Pilot skill + Ship's Thrust score

The commander may also make a Tactics (naval) check at battle start — the Effect is added to the ship's Initiative. Ships with better pilots and faster drives act first each round.

Surprise

Difficult to achieve in space — few places to hide. Damaged sensors, inattentive operators, asteroids, or feigning neutrality can allow one ship to approach undetected. A surprised ship cannot take any actions in the first round.

All positions and weapon ranges in space combat use Range Bands. Most encounters begin at Very Long or Distant range. Actual combat typically starts at Long or Medium once ships close. Core Rulebook p.165

Facing & Fire Arcs

Most spacecraft can spin or rotate freely and carry turret-mounted weapons with wide fire arcs — tracking facing is usually unnecessary. Exception: small craft (fighters) with fixed weapon mounts must physically point at their target, making facing relevant.

Manoeuvre Step

In Initiative order, each pilot allocates their ship's Thrust between movement (changing range) and combat manoeuvring. All remaining unspent Thrust after movement may be used as Evasive Action reactions later in the round. Core Rulebook p.165

Movement — Ship Movement Table

Combat Manoeuvring

Each remaining Thrust point (after movement) allows one of the following. Each may only be attempted once per round except Evasive Action.

Aid GunnersPilot check to start a task chain with gunners — provides a firing platform bonus.
DockingPilot check required. If unwilling target, opposed Pilot checks — docking ship suffers DM−2. Success allows boarding actions.
Evasive ActionEach unspent Thrust point allows the ship to dodge one incoming attack. The attack suffers a negative DM equal to the pilot's skill.

Reactions

Reactions are declared in response to incoming attacks — outside of normal turn order. Core Rulebook p.171

Evasive Action (Pilot)

Each point of unspent Thrust after movement and combat manoeuvring allows the ship to dodge one incoming attack. The attack suffers a negative DM equal to the pilot's skill.

Point Defence (Gunner)

A turret-mounted laser can destroy incoming missiles. The weapon cannot also attack this round. Make a Gunner (turret) check against the salvo — the Effect removes that many missiles. DM+1 with two lasers in turret; DM+2 with three.

Disperse Sand (Gunner)

A sandcaster can block incoming laser attacks. Make a Gunner (turret) check against the laser — if successful, add 1D + Effect to the ship's armour against that attack only. Uses one sand canister. Can also be directed against incoming boarders: success deals 8D damage (Ground scale) to each target in the boarding party.

Actions Step

After attacks are resolved, each crew member performs one action in Initiative order, limited to their assigned duty. Core Rulebook p.171

Damaging Spacecraft

Hull Damage
Damage = Weapon dice + Effect — Armour Protection

Remaining damage is removed from the ship's Hull. When Hull reaches zero, the ship is wrecked — totally inoperable, beyond economic repair, with no power or life support. Core Rulebook p.168

Sustained Damage

Every time a ship takes damage equal to 10% of its starting Hull, roll 2D on the Critical Hits Location table and apply a Severity 1 critical hit to that location.

Critical Hits

Triggered when an attack roll has Effect 6+ and causes damage. Roll 2D on the Location table. Severity = Effect − 5. Extra damage from critical hit effects ignores armour. Core Rulebook p.168

If the same location is hit again, use whichever Severity is higher (new or original +1). A location at Severity 6 cannot be hit further — instead it deals 6D extra damage each time.

Called Shots

At Short range or closer, a ship may attempt a called shot with direct-fire weapons (not missiles). Nominate the target location and attack at DM−2. If the attack succeeds and scores a critical hit, the attacker may choose which location is hit.

Launching Missiles

Missiles are fired in salvos — all missiles launched by a ship at a single target in the same round. A triple turret with three missile racks fires up to 3 missiles; multiple turrets contribute to the same salvo. Each turret holds 12 missiles. Core Rulebook p.172

Missiles at Adjacent or Close range lose the Smart trait — insufficient time to lock. Missile salvos have effective Thrust 10.

Detecting Missile Launch

When missiles are launched, other ships' sensor operators may immediately attempt to detect them.

Firing ship already detectedRoutine (6+) Electronics (sensors)
Firing ship not yet detectedAverage (8+) Electronics (sensors)
Every 10 full missiles in salvoDM+1 (max DM+6)

Undetected missiles may be spotted at the start of every subsequent round with an Average (8+) Electronics (sensors) check.

Countermeasures

As missiles take several rounds to arrive, track how many remain in each salvo — reduce as countermeasures take effect. Core Rulebook p.172

Electronic Warfare

During the Actions Step, a sensor operator makes a Difficult (10+) Electronics (sensors) check. The Effect removes that many missiles from a single inbound salvo. May be applied to the same salvo multiple times over multiple rounds (effects are cumulative), but only once per round per salvo regardless of how many operators are available.

Flee

The ship thrusts away from incoming missiles, increasing Range Band. Missiles have extremely long range, so outrunning them is rarely possible — but it may buy time for electronic warfare or to initiate a jump.

Point Defence

Performed as a Reaction just as a salvo is about to make its attack roll. A turret gunner makes a Gunner (turret) check — the Effect removes that many missiles from the salvo. A turret with two lasers grants DM+1; three lasers grants DM+2. A weapon used for point defence cannot also attack this round. Only once per round per gunner.

Missile Attack & Impact

Missile Attack Roll

When a salvo reaches its target it attacks in the Attack Step. The Gunner skill of the firer and range are not used as modifiers. Instead:

Each missile remaining in salvoDM+1 to attack roll
Missiles launched at Distant rangeDM−2 to attack roll
Evasive ActionApplied as normal

Impact Damage

Roll damage as for a single missile. Deduct armour as normal. Do not add the Effect to damage — instead, multiply damage by the Effect (Effect cannot exceed the number of missiles remaining in the salvo).

Dogfighting

When spacecraft are within Close or Adjacent range (10km or less), or directly engaging vehicles or Travellers, combat switches to six-second rounds using the normal vehicle combat rules. The three-step space combat sequence does not apply — use vehicle dogfight rules instead. Core Rulebook p.173

Dogfighting is automatic when hostile vessels are within close range — it does not cost an action. To escape, a ship needs greater Thrust than its enemy, or the enemy must be willing to let it leave.

Dogfight Resolution

At the start of every dogfight round, both pilots make opposed Pilot checks with the following modifiers. The winner chooses their fire arc positioning and gains DM+2 to all attack rolls; the loser suffers DM−2. A draw means neither can attack with fixed weapons.

If the dogfight continues, the prior round's winner applies the difference between that round's opposed check as a positive DM to the next.

Boarding Actions

Boarding Actions

When two ships are at Adjacent range (within 1km), marines may launch a boarding action during the Actions Step. The action takes 2D rounds to resolve. Core Rulebook p.175
